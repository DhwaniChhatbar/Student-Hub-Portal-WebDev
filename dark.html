Light / Dark Theme

Switch between Light Mode and Dark Mode. Your selected theme will be remembered even after refreshing the page.

Why Use Dark Mode?

  • Reduces eye strain during night-time usage.
  • Provides a comfortable reading experience.
  • Improves battery life on OLED displays.
  • Your preference is saved using localStorage.
